Privacy Policy for OhmKit

Last updated: August 13, 2026

1. Local calculations and saved data

OhmKit performs calculations on your device. Settings, calculation history, favorites, projects, presets, and supported backups are stored locally. OhmKit does not require an account and the developer does not operate a backend that receives this information.

2. Advertising

The free version uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Depending on your region, consent choices, and device settings, Google and its partners may process IP address and approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostic information, and advertising performance data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Where required, OhmKit requests consent before requesting ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Purchases

OhmKit Pro purchases are processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive full payment-card details. Google Play supplies purchase status and transaction-verification data so the app can unlock or restore Pro access.

4. Sharing and contact

Content you choose to share is passed to the selected application through the system share sheet. Contact, suggestion, and error-report actions open your email application; a message is sent only after you choose to send it.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it in OhmKit, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Ad and purchase data is retained according to the policies of Google and the applicable app store.

6. Children

OhmKit is a general-purpose engineering utility and is not directed to children.

7. Security and changes

Data handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. No transmission or storage method is completely secure. This policy may be updated when OhmKit or its third-party services change.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: ohmkitapp@gmail.com
