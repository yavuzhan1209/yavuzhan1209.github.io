Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Scope

This policy explains how RollKit, a photo and video organization application, handles information. RollKit does not require an account and the RollKit developer does not operate a backend that receives your photo or video library.

2. Photo and video access

With your permission, RollKit reads the media that the operating system makes available so you can review, organize, play, place in albums, and request deletion of items. Media analysis and previews are performed on the device. Photo and video contents are not uploaded to the RollKit developer or used to personalize ads.

3. Information stored on the device

RollKit stores settings, language choice, onboarding status, review decisions, cleanup statistics, daily free-use counters, rewarded-ad rewards, and Pro entitlement state locally. You can remove this data by clearing the app data or uninstalling RollKit.

4. Advertising

Free users may voluntarily watch rewarded ads provided by Google Mobile Ads to receive additional decisions. Google and its partners may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, diagnostics, consent choices, and ad interactions under their own policies. They do not receive your photo or video files from RollKit. Availability of an ad is not guaranteed.

5. Purchases

RollKit Pro purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple App Store. The store may process payment, account, transaction, tax, and device information under its own policy. RollKit receives product and transaction status needed to unlock or restore Pro; the RollKit developer does not receive your card details.

6. User-initiated sharing and third parties

When you share a highlights card, the file leaves RollKit only after you choose a destination in the system share interface. That destination handles it under its own policy. Operating-system photo libraries, app stores, Google Mobile Ads, and the apps you select to share with are independent services.

7. Retention, deletion, and control

Local RollKit data remains until you clear it or uninstall the app. A media deletion request is handled by the operating system and may first move an item to Recently Deleted or Trash. You can revoke media permission in system settings. Data processed independently by Google or Apple is retained and controlled under their policies.

8. Children, security, changes, and contact

RollKit is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. We use the platform security controls available to the app, but no system is risk-free. This policy may change when features or data practices change; the date above identifies the current version. Questions may be sent to support.mesaitakip@gmail.com.
